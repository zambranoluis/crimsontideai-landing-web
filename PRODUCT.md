## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary.** Companies, organisations and institutions — in Jamaica, the Caribbean and beyond — evaluating whether to buy AI software or commission it. They arrive from a referral, a search or a conversation, needing to establish two things quickly: that CrimsonTide is a real software company with delivered work behind it, and which of its two independent products or its tailored engagements fits the problem they came with.

**Secondary.** Partners and allies, prospective talent, press, the wider technology ecosystem, and public-sector bodies interested in technology developed in Jamaica.

The audiences do not share a proposition. Each should reach products, solutions, evidence, company and contact without reading the others' material.

## Product Purpose

The CrimsonTide corporate website. It introduces the company, routes each visitor to the right product or engagement, demonstrates delivered experience, and makes starting a conversation easy.

It is not a product experience. OpenJM and Sentinel have their own properties; this site hands visitors off to them.

**Primary conversion:** a contact enquiry.

**Microconversions:** explore OpenJM; explore Sentinel; review tailored solutions; read the case study; check sector relevance; read about the company.

The visitor path the site is built around: discovers products and solutions → understands experience and credibility → contacts CrimsonTide.

## Positioning

CrimsonTide is an AI software company, built in Jamaica.

It is a **Product + Solutions** company, and that combination is the differentiated position. It ships its own independent software products *and* designs, builds, adapts and integrates software around a client's specific needs. A consultancy cannot point to products it shipped; a single-product vendor cannot rebuild its platform around one organisation's problem.

The three parts of the statement each carry weight: **AI** is core development expertise, not a label; **software company** means products, platforms and implementations rather than advice alone; **built in Jamaica** is origin, identity, local technological capability and regional credibility.

Capability comes first; origin is a strength, never the substitute for one.

CrimsonTide must not be positioned as a security company, as "the Sentinel company", as "the RAHKIA company", as a development agency, as an AI consultancy, or as a company that is interesting only because it is Jamaican.

**Proprietary products** — currently OpenJM and Sentinel. Both were developed by CrimsonTide, and that is the whole of their shared corporate relationship. They are independent products with different brand identities, audiences, problems, sectors, commercial models and conversion paths.

**Solutions and services** — tailored AI solutions, custom software development, adaptation of existing products into customised experiences, integrations, implementations, and projects designed around a particular need.

## Capabilities and Constraints

CrimsonTide designs proprietary software products, develops artificial intelligence solutions, develops custom software for clients, implements and integrates solutions, and adapts existing products into customised experiences.

**RAHKIA is not an independent product.** OpenJM replaced RAHKIA as the product name; RAHKIA is now the main AI model inside OpenJM. It is never presented as a product of its own.

**The site does not carry product depth.** Pricing, onboarding, model catalogues, full FAQs, support and technical documentation belong to the product properties, not here.

**Language:** English. One locale — no locale routing, no language switcher, no translated copy.

Undecided, and not to be filled in by inference:

- publishable clients beyond the three named below;
- detailed sector positioning;
- publishable patents, testimonials, or any verifiable performance claim;
- final legal policies for the corporate entity — Privacy, Terms and Support are named in the footer but have no destination.

## Brand Commitments

**Name:** CrimsonTide, one word, capital C and T. **Legal entity:** CrimsonTide AI Limited, used in the footer.

**Reference statement:** *AI software company, built in Jamaica.*

**Voice:** clear, direct, professional and understandable. Confident and approachable without informality; specialised without unnecessary jargon. Capability is explained before jargon is used.

**Product brands stay independent.** OpenJM must not inherit CrimsonTide's personality, Sentinel's visual identity, Sentinel's sectors, or the company's corporate narrative. Sentinel must not be confused with CrimsonTide, with OpenJM, or with the general solutions practice.

**Jamaica supports the proposition; it never replaces it.** No dependence on flags, tourism imagery or geographic cliché.

## Product Principles

1. **Capability first; origin as a strength.** Jamaica strengthens the proposition and never stands in for it.
2. **The company is not a product.** CrimsonTide is what builds OpenJM, Sentinel and client software — never one of them by another name.
3. **Products stay independent.** No shared functional narrative is forced between OpenJM and Sentinel; the site explains that CrimsonTide builds different products for different problems.
4. **Evidence stops where confirmation stops.** Claims are bounded by what is documented; an absence is stated, not filled.
5. **Route, don't broadcast.** Each region of the site addresses one audience with one primary action, rather than selling every proposition to everyone.
