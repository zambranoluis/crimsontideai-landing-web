Globe artwork credits

The CrimsonTide 404 globe and its static poster combine geographic maps with original lighting, signals, and orbital artwork.

  • Earth normal and night maps: Solar System Scope / INOVE, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. The 2K normal TIFF was converted losslessly to PNG. The night JPEG is unchanged. In the renderer, the maps are reprojected onto the globe, with amplified relief, cool-white night lights, a dark metallic palette, and crimson illumination. The static globe poster includes this adapted material.
  • Land mask: Natural Earth 1:50m physical land polygons, public domain, rasterized for the globe.
  • Globe lighting, grid, signals, orbits, and terrain: original procedural artwork by CrimsonTide.
