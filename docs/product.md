## Reference statement

> AI software company, built in Jamaica.

This is the corporate reference formulation. It is a positioning statement, not automatically final public copy.

The internal working synthesis of the whole project is:

> CrimsonTide is an AI software company, built in Jamaica. It develops independent software products such as OpenJM and Sentinel, while also creating tailored AI solutions, custom software and product adaptations for organisations. Its corporate website should introduce the company, connect visitors with the right product or solution, demonstrate real experience and credibility, and ultimately make it easy to start a conversation with CrimsonTide.

This synthesis is internal and not automatically final public copy.

## What CrimsonTide is

CrimsonTide is a software company specialising in artificial intelligence, founded in Jamaica.

CrimsonTide must not be understood as a specific product. It is the company that:

- designs proprietary software products;
- develops artificial intelligence solutions;
- develops custom software for clients;
- implements and integrates solutions;
- can adapt existing products into customised experiences according to a client's needs.

## Company model

CrimsonTide is a **Product + Solutions company**. Its activity divides into two areas.

### Proprietary products

The current primary products are **OpenJM** and **Sentinel**. Both:

- were developed by CrimsonTide;
- are independent products;
- have different brand identities;
- have different audiences;
- solve different problems;
- serve different sectors;
- may use different commercial models and conversion paths.

Their only common relationship is: _developed by CrimsonTide_.

### Solutions and services

CrimsonTide also works directly with clients through:

- tailored artificial intelligence solutions;
- custom software development;
- adaptation of existing products;
- integrations;
- implementations;
- configuration of specific experiences;
- projects designed around particular needs.

## Brand architecture

```text
CrimsonTide
|
+-- OpenJM
|   +-- RAHKIA - main artificial intelligence model
|
+-- Sentinel
|
+-- AI Solutions & Software Development
```

**CrimsonTide** represents company, technological capability, vision, track record, experience, people, credibility, products, solutions, and origin in Jamaica.

**OpenJM** is an independent product brand. It must not automatically adopt CrimsonTide's personality, Sentinel's visual identity, Sentinel's sectors, or CrimsonTide's complete corporate narrative.

**Sentinel** is an independent product brand. It must not be confused with CrimsonTide, with OpenJM, or with the company's general custom solutions.

### RAHKIA migration rule

RAHKIA is **no longer an independent product**. It is the main artificial intelligence model inside OpenJM. OpenJM replaced RAHKIA as the product name. Never present RAHKIA as an independent product.

## Strategic difference between the products

OpenJM and Sentinel must not be presented as variants of the same product. The corporate website explains that _CrimsonTide develops different products for different problems_; it does not need to force a shared functional narrative between them.

## Corporate purpose

CrimsonTide exists to:

- develop software and artificial intelligence products from Jamaica;
- create useful tools and solutions;
- turn real problems into technological products and systems;
- drive artificial intelligence adoption;
- bring technological capability from Jamaica and the Caribbean;
- develop technology with regional relevance and global potential.

This is a strategic foundation, not necessarily final website copy.

## Positioning

### What the reference statement communicates

- **AI** — artificial intelligence is part of the company's core expertise and development activity.
- **Software company** — CrimsonTide builds products, develops software, creates platforms, implements solutions, and is not limited to consulting.
- **Built in Jamaica** — origin, identity, local technological capability, context, and regional credibility.

### What must be avoided

CrimsonTide must not be positioned only as:

- "a security company";
- "the Sentinel company";
- "the RAHKIA company";
- "a development agency";
- "an AI consultancy";
- "a company relevant only because it is Jamaican".

## Role of Jamaica

Jamaica functions as origin, identity element, proof of local capability, company context, and differentiator.

It must not replace utility, experience, technological capability, or results.

The conceptual rule is: **capability first; origin as a strength.**

## Audiences

**Primary commercial audience:** companies, organisations, institutions, potential software clients, potential AI solution clients, and buyers interested in OpenJM or Sentinel.

**Secondary audiences:** partners, allies, talent, press, the technology ecosystem, the public sector, and organisations interested in innovation developed in Jamaica.

**Communication rule:** the website must not try to sell the same proposition to everyone. Each audience should quickly find products, solutions, evidence, company, and contact.

## Conversion

The defined primary path is:

> Visitor → discovers products and solutions → understands experience and credibility → contacts CrimsonTide

**Primary conversion:** contact CrimsonTide.

**Microconversions available in the current scope:** explore OpenJM; explore Sentinel; review solutions; view the case study; review industries; review clients and partnerships; read about the company.

Its stated objectives are security, loss prevention, operational visibility, and information supporting decisions related to operations and customer experience.

**Sector rule:** these sectors are strongly associated with Sentinel. They must not automatically be presented as the entirety of CrimsonTide's industries.
